========================================
              INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

The game consists of FIVE locations and TEN interactable characters. 
Through interacting with them and passing their tests, player can gain clues. 
Clues will lead what to do next, where to go. 
Clues are crucial till the end of the game. 

Player can start from any location and solve any task they like. 
But to trigger the final showdown, all the clues must be obtained.

Remember, your CHOICE matters.
