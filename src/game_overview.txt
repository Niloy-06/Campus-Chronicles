========================================
             ABOUT THE GAME
========================================

THE CAMPUS CHRONICLES

The Campus Chronicles is a short,
story-driven mystery adventure set
around the KUET campus.

A few hours before the annual cultural
show, the keys to the auditorium
mysteriously disappear.

Without the keys, the auditorium and
its control room cannot be opened.

Three students are chosen to investigate
the mystery.

Follow the clues, question suspicious
characters, solve puzzles, and make
decisions that shape your investigation.

But be careful...

Three clues are true.
Two are traps.

Can your team recover the missing keys
before the show begins?
